About this chit box

A chit box is a devotional practice: you sit quietly, think of Swami, hold your worry in your heart and draw a chit. This page keeps that box with you anywhere, any time, so you can pause, pray and reflect, even on a hard day.

This is a devotee’s collection of 230 chit messages, offered for prayer and reflection. It is not an official source. The photo with each chit is chosen to match its message. For health, safety, money or legal matters, please also speak with a doctor, a trusted elder or a qualified professional.
